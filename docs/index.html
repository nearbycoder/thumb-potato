        Features

        Find it, play it, finish it.

        One app for the whole backlog, from what's out this week to the game you finally finish on Sunday.

        • Tonight

          New releases, games coming soon, top picks for your platforms, Steam's best sellers and the best PC deals. Open the app and something good is already waiting.

        • Backlog

          Log hours and progress, mark what you own, rate anything out of five, and see how many games you've beaten this year.

          • Want to Play
          • Playing
          • Beaten
          • Shelved

        • Release Radar

          Follow a game that isn't out yet and it lands on your radar with a countdown. A release-day alert arrives at 9 AM.

          • Planner

            Plan play sessions on a week strip, add them to your calendar and get a reminder fifteen minutes before you're due on the couch.

          • Shuffle

            Can't choose? Set a mood and how long you have, from a quick break to a long session, then let the deck pick. Shuffle again if fate gets it wrong.

          • Where to Play

            Store links for every platform a game is on, yours first, plus live PC prices from across the major stores.

          • Moods

            Browse by how you feel instead of by genre. Each mood is its own lane of games that fit it, on the platforms you own.

          • Sync

            Apple devices sync through your own iCloud, with no account to create. Android exports and imports the same backup file, so your backlog moves with you.

          Apps

          At home on every screen.

          Native SwiftUI on iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo and Mac, and a Jetpack Compose app for Android phones, tablets and foldables.

          Data

          No keys required.

          Thumb Potato runs on open sources out of the box. Every request goes straight from your device to the source; there is no Thumb Potato server in between.

          • W

            Wikipedia & Wikidata

            The catalog, search across every game ever made, box art and descriptions.

            Text CC BY-SA. Wikidata CC0.

          • ST

            Steam

            Library art and backdrops, top sellers, reviews and Steam Deck status for PC and Mac games.

            Valve's public store data.

          • CS

            CheapShark

            Live PC prices across stores, and the Deals row on Tonight.

            Free public API.

          • RG

            RAWG Optional

            Paste a free key in Settings → Data Sources for endless browsing and deeper search.

            Get a key

          The catalog holds the 1,200 most popular games on each platform, rebuilt every week from Wikidata, Wikipedia and Steam and served from GitHub Pages.

          Build

          Build it yourself.

          Thumb Potato is open source. Clone the repository and run it on your own devices in a few minutes.

          Apple

          iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo and Mac

          • Xcode26 or later
          • iOS / iPadOS26 or later
          • macOS26 or later
          • XcodeGenvia Homebrew
          brew install xcodegengit clone https://github.com/nearbycoder/thumb-potatocd thumb-potatoxcodegenopen ThumbPotato.xcodeproj

          Pick the ThumbPotato scheme, choose a device and run.

          Android

          Phones, tablets and foldables

          • Android StudioLadybug or later
          • JDK17 or later
          cd thumb-potato/android./gradlew :app:installDebug

          Or open the android/ folder in Android Studio.

          Optional: add a free RAWG API key in Settings → Data Sources for infinite scrolling on platform pages and RAWG-powered search. Without one the app uses the catalog, Wikipedia, Steam and CheapShark.
