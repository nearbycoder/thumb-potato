    Sync

    One backlog on every device.

    Add a game on your iPhone and it's on your iPad and Mac a moment later. Your backlog, hours, ratings and sessions sync through iCloud, with no account to create.

    Up to date

    iCloud, automatically

    Every iPhone, iPad and Mac on your Apple ID shares one backlog. Settings shows when it last synced.

    Android, too

    Export your backlog and import it on the other side. It merges, and the newest changes win.

    Private by design

    No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your backlog lives on your devices and in your own iCloud.

    Build

    Run it yourself.

    Thumb Potato is open source. Clone the repository and have it on your own devices in a few minutes.

    Apple

    iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo and Mac

    • Xcode26 or later
    • iOS / iPadOS26 or later
    • macOS26 or later
    • XcodeGenvia Homebrew
    brew install xcodegengit clone https://github.com/nearbycoder/thumb-potatocd thumb-potatoxcodegenopen ThumbPotato.xcodeproj

    Android

    Phones, tablets and foldables

    • Android StudioLadybug or later
    • JDK17 or later
    cd thumb-potato/android./gradlew :app:installDebug

    No keys required. Out of the box, Thumb Potato uses its own weekly catalog built from Wikidata, Wikipedia and Steam, plus live data from Wikipedia, Steam and CheapShark. Add a free RAWG API key in Settings → Data Sources for live search and browsing across RAWG's full database.
